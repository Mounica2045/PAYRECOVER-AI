# PayRecover AI

AI-powered payment recovery intelligence for merchants.

## 🚀 Overview

PayRecover AI helps merchants identify failed-payment recovery opportunities, understand failure patterns, recommend recovery strategies, and safely simulate recovery workflows.

## 🎯 Problem

Failed payments can result in significant revenue loss. Merchants often lack intelligent tools to identify which payments are worth attempting to recover and which recovery strategy should be used.

## 💡 Solution

PayRecover AI combines:

- AI Recovery Intelligence
- Recovery Probability
- Strategy Recommendations
- Strategy Simulation
- Recovery Campaigns
- Safety Checks
- Merchant Approval
- Audit Logging
- AI Agent Orchestration

## ✨ Key Features

### Dashboard
Merchant-level payment health and revenue-at-risk insights.

### Payments
Searchable and filterable failed-payment transactions.

### AI Recovery
Analyzes payment failures and identifies recovery opportunities.

### Strategy Simulator
Compares potential recovery strategies.

### Recovery Campaigns
Creates simulated recovery campaigns.

### AI Intelligence
Provides predictive insights and recovery analytics.

### AI Agent
Allows merchants to interact with the recovery system using natural-language commands.

### Safety & Audit
Provides approval gates, safety checks, simulation mode, and audit trails.

## 🔐 Safety

This project is designed as a hackathon/demo application.

No real financial transactions are executed.

All recovery actions are simulated.

No sensitive payment credentials such as CVV, PIN, or full card numbers are stored.
